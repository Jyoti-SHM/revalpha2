Revenue Management for Boutique Hotels

At a boutique property, pricing risk cuts both ways. Price wrong and rooms stay empty, discount too hard and you weaken your brand. Guests choose you for your character and your reviews, not a chain name. RevAlpha, the hotel revenue management service by Hospitality Minds, prices you against the few properties you truly compete with and turns your reputation into more direct bookings.

What’s included?

Everything we manage for you.

Here is exactly what RevAlpha takes on for a boutique hotels property.

Premium pricing strategy

We price rooms to match your premium positioning, not just to fill beds.

AI-powered market demand

Our AI-assisted tool reads market demand and competitor moves, so your rates stay competitive without checking every OTA by hand.

AI-powered compset tracking

We compare your rate with boutique properties truly like yours nearby, and use AI forecasting to see demand before it arrives.

ADR protection

We avoid deep discounts that hurt how guests see your property.

Direct booking incentives

Direct-rate offers and return-guest deals that bring more bookings straight to you instead of through an OTA.

AI-powered ORM with fast review replies

Our AI tools track your reviews and reply for you where enabled, because for a boutique hotel, reputation is the brand.

Weekly reports and review meetings

A weekly revenue report, plus review meetings twice a month and a monthly planning meeting.

Curated experience upsell

Ideas to sell what makes you different, like a signature breakfast, a local experience or a character room upgrade.
