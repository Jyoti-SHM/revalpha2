Multi-Property Portfolio

Revenue Management for Hotel Groups

Managing revenue one property at a time doesn’t scale past a handful of hotels, and a portfolio is rarely one property type. RevAlpha, the hotel revenue management service by Hospitality Minds, brings the same pricing and forecasting as our single-property plans to your whole group. We adjust the approach per property, with business-hotel demand forecasting for city hotels and seasonal and festival pricing for resorts, and report everything in one place.

What’s included?

Everything we manage for you.

Here is exactly what RevAlpha takes on for a group of hotels property.

Portfolio-wide demand forecasting

We forecast demand for every property, so pricing stays consistent across the group.

Portfolio-wide B2B discount optimization

B2B and corporate discounts are managed consistently, so no hotel gives away more than needed.

Conference and exhibition tracking

We track events near each business hotel and raise rates on the dates that matter.

Seasonal and festival demand monitoring

For your resorts, we track school holidays, festivals and long weekends and price ahead of them.

ADR tracking vs competitors

Each property’s average rate is compared with its own local competitors and rolled up into one view.

Weekend and last-minute demand management

Slower weekends and last-minute bookings managed consistently across the group.

Ancillary revenue optimization

For resort properties, we help you earn more from meals, spa and activities, included in group reporting.

Weekly and monthly portfolio reports

One report format across every property, so you can compare performance side by side.

AI-driven ORM reports

We track online reviews and reputation across every property with AI-assisted tools and share the key insights.

AI-assisted pricing, compset and forecasting

Our AI tools support dynamic pricing, competitor tracking and forecasting across your whole portfolio.
