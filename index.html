Where revenue leaks

Three leaks quietly drain independent hotels.

Most hotels do not have a demand problem. They have a pricing and visibility problem that repeats every week, in the same three places.

01

Rates set once, then left alone

Static rates miss demand peaks and quietly discount into weak dates. The calendar fills, but the rate never follows demand.

See the pattern
02

Occupancy bought with cheap rooms

Filling rooms at any price erodes RevPAR. High occupancy on a soft rate is not the same as a good month.

See the pattern
03

No visibility on pickup

By the time the week is reviewed, the opportunity has passed. Pickup has to be watched daily, not remembered monthly.

See the pattern

Scope, in plain terms

What’s included, and what isn’t.

Included

In every revenue management plan

  • A dedicated revenue manager for your property
  • Daily rate review on working days
  • Pickup and pace monitoring
