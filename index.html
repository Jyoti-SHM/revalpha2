Why hotels choose RevAlpha.

Block 01

You need better revenue decisions.

If your rates change at random, your OTAs run without a clear strategy, or you only see performance after the month is over, you are leaving money on the table. RevAlpha gives you a clear revenue management system for hotels: what is happening, why it is happening and what to do next.

01

Stop selling too cheap

We track demand, pickup, competitor rates and key local dates, so your strongest nights are sold at the right price, not discounted.

02

Fill weak dates intelligently

No panic discounting. We find the right mix of price, channel, offer and timing to lift occupancy on slow dates.

03

Know where your revenue comes from

See OTA contribution, ADR, occupancy, RevPAR, channel mix and upcoming revenue opportunities in one clear view.

Included / Not included

What’s included, and what isn’t.

We would rather be clear up front than surprise you later. Here is exactly what our revenue management service covers.

Included

What RevAlpha owns

  • Revenue strategy and dynamic pricing recommendations
  • OTA management and distribution actions within agreed access
  • Forecasting, pickup and pace analysis
  • AI-driven competitor and market intelligence
  • Revenue opportunity identification
  • Performance reporting and strategy reviews
  • Proactive alerts on important revenue dates

Not included

What stays outside RevAlpha?

  • Guest reservations or front-office operations
  • Guest complaint handling
  • Corporate / hotel sales, unless agreed separately
  • Social media, SEO, website or paid ads (available through Hospitality Minds)
  • OTA commission accounting / reconciliation
  • PMS, channel manager or RMS subscription fees
  • Guaranteed occupancy, ADR or revenue growth
